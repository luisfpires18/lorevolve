LorEvolve

Privacy note

This covers the anonymous survey and the visit counts on this page, and nothing else. Version 4, November 2026.

Who reads the answers

LorEvolve is a one-person project. I'm the only person who reads what you send, and you can reach me at support@lorevolve.com.

What is kept

Only the answers you choose to give, and the day they arrived (not the time). The survey asks for no name and no email, and your IP address, browser and device are not stored, so an answer can't be traced back to you. Please leave names and contact details out of the free-text answers too.

Why

To help decide what to build next and how LorEvolve could be priced. Nothing else. The answers are never sold or shared, and there's no advertising on this page.

Where it lives

The page and the answers are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is sending the survey; it doesn't use cookies to do that.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to this page: which pages are opened, the site that linked here, and the country, browser and device type. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you across sites, and isn't linked to anything you send in the survey.

The page also counts, on its own, each visit, each feature or button you click and each video you play, with the day only. To tell people from bots it notes whether a mouse, key or touch was used. No IP address, cookie or browser details are kept, so a count can't be traced to you.

How long

Until the alpha ends, then the answers are deleted.

Your choices

Every question is optional, and you can close the survey at any point without sending anything. Because nothing ties an answer to you, a sent answer can't be found again to show you or delete on request.
