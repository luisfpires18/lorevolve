LorEvolve

Privacy note

This covers the alpha application form on this page, and nothing else. Version 1, October 2026.

Who keeps your answers

LorEvolve is a one-person project. I'm the only person who reads what you send, and you can reach me at lorevolve@gmail.com.

What is kept

Your email address, your name, what you'd use LorEvolve for, the time you agreed to be contacted, and any survey answers you chose to give. If you send the form again, it is kept as a new entry next to the earlier one; nothing you sent is overwritten. Your IP address, browser and device are not stored.

Why

To choose who gets into the alpha, to email you an invitation and news about the alpha, and to help decide how LorEvolve could be priced. Nothing else. It is never sold or shared, and there's no advertising or tracking on this page.

Where it lives

The page and your answers are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is sending the form; it doesn't use cookies to do that.

How long

Until the alpha ends, then it is deleted. If you get an invitation and create an account, that account is covered by LorEvolve's own terms instead.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what you sent, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, at any time and without giving a reason. Email lorevolve@gmail.com from the same address and I'll do it. Agreeing to be contacted is your choice; you can withdraw it the same way.
